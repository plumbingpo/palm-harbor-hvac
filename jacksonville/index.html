Keeping Your Jacksonville HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Take a look at the age of your system. That number tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and also shows rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement in peak season.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is.
